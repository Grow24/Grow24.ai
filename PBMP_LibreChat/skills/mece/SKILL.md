---
name: mece
description: Structure any business problem into mutually exclusive, collectively exhaustive branches before analysing or recommending.
---

# MECE Analysis

- Split the problem so branches do not overlap and together cover the decision.
- Typical splits: geography, customer segment, cost vs revenue, risk vs return, now vs later.
- Name each branch, then analyse it with evidence.
- Do not bury a recommendation inside mixed commentary; close with one MECE map of options.
