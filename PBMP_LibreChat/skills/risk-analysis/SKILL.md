# Risk Analysis

- List the top risks with severity and why they matter financially or operationally.
- Distinguish open vs proposed new risks.
- If asked to update a PBMP risk register, summarise the proposed write and wait for human approval before calling create_risk or update_risk.
- Close with recommended mitigations in priority order.
