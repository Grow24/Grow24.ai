# PBMP LibreChat — flow plan (non-technical)

This is the simple version. You do not need to know code.

Open the daily screen: **http://localhost:5200/PBMP_LibreChat/**

---

## The one flow (every question)

1. **You ask** — type a business question, or tap a ready-made one.
2. **Meaning** — PBMP first understands *what kind of work* this is (dashboard, profit, acquisition, India markets…).
3. **Main helper stays in charge** — it does not fake five voices in one reply.
4. **Specialist helpers go out** — research, finance, risk, and others, each with their own job. Sometimes they use a different AI brain.
5. **Numbers and company data** — maths goes through a calculator; customer / sales / contract lists come from PBMP data, not from a guess.
6. **Canvas on the right** — a live report, table, chart, dashboard, map, or 3D view. You can click and filter.
7. **One combined answer on the left** — the main helper writes the recommendation.

The app is the **control room**. The AI (GPT-5.6 Sol, when a key is attached) is the **thinking**. Same idea as a well-run office.

## Seven ready questions

Tap these on Workspace. Each is a different journey.

**A. Sales dashboard**  
*“Create a sales dashboard showing pipeline by region, monthly sales, conversion rate, and let me switch between products.”*  
→ Live board with a product switch. Not a picture of a dashboard.

**B. Why profit fell**  
*“Analyse these numbers and tell me why profitability has deteriorated.”*  
→ Finance runs the numbers. Risk reads contracts. You get one executive brief plus a chart.

**C. Acquisition**  
*“Prepare acquisition analysis”*  
→ Outside research + finance + risk, then one memo.

**D. India markets (full journey)**  
*“Find the five most attractive markets for our product in India and create an investment recommendation.”*  
→ Web + our coverage + a model → written rec + dashboard + map + 3D.

**E. Fixed review chain**  
*“Run the Research → Analysis → Critic → Report chain.”*  
→ Four steps in a fixed order, like a quality gate.

**F. Split a large job**  
*“This task is large. Create isolated copies of yourself to analyse customer, competition, financials and risks.”*  
→ Four separate looks, then one combined view.

**G. Do not guess the maths**  
*“Do not calculate this mentally — run Python / Code Interpreter.”*  
→ A numbers helper plus a real calculator.

## Ready now vs later

**Ready now:** all seven flows, live canvas, helpers splitting work, sample company data, calculator. The process runs even without an AI key.

**Later (a business decision):** attach GPT-5.6 Sol for ChatGPT-like thinking quality; connect live company systems; save long history; add video/audio as extras.

---

## The rule this is built to keep

PBMP should not become “just send the question to an AI”.

- PBMP keeps **meaning, templates, and data**
- The chat layer **organises helpers**
- The AI **thinks**, when it is attached
