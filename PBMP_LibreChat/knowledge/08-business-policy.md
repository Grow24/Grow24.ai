# Business policy / SOP — market entry

1. A market may be recommended for first launch only if last-12-month ROI is at least 18% **or** residual risk is Low.
2. Writes to PBMP (requirements, project status, risks) require a human to confirm the proposed payload first.
3. External market claims must be labelled as external; internal figures must come from RAG or PBMP tools.
4. Management output should include a recommendation sequence, not a list of “considerations”.
